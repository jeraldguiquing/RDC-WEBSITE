SDG Advocacies: Research in the Service of Communities

Bridging academic research, clinical expertise, and legal scholarship directly into tangible healthcare, public safety, and environmental outreach for Cagayan Valley.

Translational Research

The Ronald & Cristina Guzman Legacy of Social Responsibility

At MCNP and ISAP, research does not end on the printed pages of scientific journals. Under the SDG Advocacies initiative, scientific discoveries, health protocols, and criminology analyses are translated into active community development programs that directly benefit underserved barangays across Peñablanca, Tuguegarao City, and rural municipalities in Cagayan Valley.

In keeping with the institutional ideals of founder Dr. Ronald P. Guzman and Atty. Cristina Guzman, the SDG Advocacies integrate community extension into the academic curricula of all colleges, pairing empirical research with compassionate humanitarian action.

Flagship SDG Extension Programs

1. Alagang Dr. Guzman (SDG) Health Caravans

MCNP Medical Wing

Providing free comprehensive medical consultations, complete blood counts, urinalysis, radiologic chest screenings, and prescription medicines to remote upland barangays in Peñablanca and indigenous Agta communities.

Research Linkage: Real-time epidemiological data collection on prevalent non-communicable diseases and nutritional deficiencies in upland Cagayan.

2. Community Legal Literacy & Crime Prevention Clinics

ISAP Criminology & Customs

Faculty-led workshops educating barangay tanods, youth leaders, and women's cooperatives on domestic violence reporting (RA 9262), cyber safety against online sexual abuse of children (OSAEC), and dispute mediation.

Research Linkage: Empirical baseline surveys measuring community legal comprehension and dispute resolution efficacy.

3. Pinacanauan & Cagayan River Ecological Stewardship

Joint Environmental Wing

Collaborative tree-planting campaigns along riverbank buffer zones, continuous water quality microbiological sampling, and community solid waste segregation seminars.

Research Linkage: Water quality indices published in the MCNP Health Journal to advise the Peñablanca Municipal Environment Office.

4. Barangay Health Worker (BHW) Skills Enhancement

Continuing Education

Accredited hands-on training modules delivered by nursing and medical technology faculty to upskill frontline community health volunteers in vital signs monitoring, maternal danger sign triage, and infectious disease reporting.

Research Linkage: Assessing long-term clinical skill retention among rural healthcare volunteers.
