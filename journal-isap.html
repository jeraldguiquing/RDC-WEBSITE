Articles in this Issue

Research Article • Criminal Justice

Predictive Profiling and Community Policing Frameworks in Cagayan Valley Rural Municipalities

R. S. Duldulao, Ph.D. Crim; M. L. Soriano, CSP; J. V. Mallillin, MSIT

Evaluated predictive analytical models applied to five years of barangay blotter datasets in Cagayan Valley. Findings demonstrate that proactive neighborhood watch training combined with GIS hot-spot reporting reduced property crimes by 34% over a 24-month monitoring window.

DOI: 10.5281/isap.2026.1104 • Pages 1–16
Research Article • Information Technology & Forensics

Cryptographic Hash Validation and Chain-of-Custody Integrity in Mobile Forensics Investigations

E. V. Pasion, DIT; B. K. Tolentino, MSCS

Proposes an automated blockchain-anchored ledger protocol for preserving digital forensic evidence acquired from suspect cellular devices during Philippine National Police cyber-crime raids, guaranteeing tamper-proof verification in judicial trials.

DOI: 10.5281/isap.2026.1105 • Pages 17–31
Research Article • Customs Administration

Post-Clearance Audit Compliance and Revenue Leakage Prevention at Northern Luzon Maritime Ports

Atty. K. M. Bautista, LCB, MBA; G. N. Ramirez, PhD

Investigated post-clearance audit mechanisms at Port of Irene and Port of Aparri, measuring the deterrence effect of real-time electronic declaration audits on tariff misclassification among regional industrial importers.

DOI: 10.5281/isap.2026.1106 • Pages 32–48
Research Article • Teacher Education & Pedagogy

Cognitive Engagement and Critical Thinking in Simulation-Based Law Enforcement Training

Prof. H. J. Almazan, EdD; C. R. Guzman, PhD

Assessed the tactical decision-making accuracy of 180 criminology interns subjected to high-stress shoot/don't-shoot simulated virtual environments compared to traditional lecture-based firearm instruction protocols.

DOI: 10.5281/isap.2026.1107 • Pages 49–64
