MCNP-ISAP RDC

RDC Joins CBCP-UST Collaborative Project on Catholic Christianity

The MCNP-ISAP Research and Development Center, led by Research Director Ryan C. Fontanilla, participated in the CBCP and University of Santo Tomas project at the Archbishop’s Residence in Tuguegarao City.

Photo 1 placeholder
Photo 2 placeholder
Photo 3 placeholder
Read full story

The initiative assesses the current status of Catholic Christianity in the Philippines toward fostering a Synodal Church. Lay organizations across the Archdiocese of Tuguegarao answered the project survey, while respondents’ privacy was protected by blurring faces in event photographs.
