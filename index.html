Academic Synergy

The Twin Institutions of Cagayan Valley

Rooted on the historic crest of Alimannao Hills, Peñablanca, MCNP and ISAP unite medical precision with multidisciplinary innovation under the visionary founding leadership of the late Dr. Ronald P. Guzman and Atty. Cristina Guzman.

Medical & Allied Health Sciences

Medical Colleges of Northern Philippines

Pioneering premier healthcare education, laboratory diagnostics, and clinical research in Northern Luzon.

Disciplines: Nursing, Medical Technology, Pharmacy, Radiologic Technology, Physical Therapy.
Research Scope: Infectious disease surveillance, community public health, pharmacognosy, clinical innovations.
Flagship Publication: MCNP Journal of Medical and Allied Health Sciences.

Strategic Direction

Institutional Research Agenda

Aligned with the National Higher Education Research Agenda (NHERA), DOST Harmonized National R&D Agenda, and the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

01
🩺

Healthcare Innovation & Epidemic Preparedness

Clinical epidemiology, rural healthcare delivery models, endemic pathogen surveillance, diagnostic radiography enhancements.

02
🛡️

Public Safety, Forensic Science & Criminology

Crime prevention strategies, digital forensics, penal management innovations, comparative jurisprudence in Southeast Asia.

03
💻

Technological Transformation & AI Integration

Health informatics, intelligent diagnostic assistants, e-learning systems, algorithmic data security for regional organizations.

04
🌱

Community Health, Environment & Sustainability

Cagayan River basin eco-studies, disaster risk reduction and climate resilience, nutrition and indigenous wellness solutions.

05
📚

Educational Pedagogy & Curricular Reforms

Outcome-based medical education, simulation laboratory impact studies, blended learning assessments, teacher development.

06
📊

Business Governance & Customs Optimization

Cross-border customs trade facilitation, SME economic resilience in Region II, corporate ethics, digital logistics management.
