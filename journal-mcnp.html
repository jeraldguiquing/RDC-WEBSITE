Articles in this Issue

Research Article • Medical Laboratory Science

Assessment of Antimicrobial Activity of Selected Indigenous Flora in Peñablanca, Cagayan Against Clinically Isolated Pathogens

A. R. Pascual, RMT, MSMLS; D. C. Agbisit, MD; E. K. Guzman, PharmD

This study investigated the phytochemical constituents and in vitro antibacterial efficacy of crude ethanolic and aqueous leaf extracts of three indigenous plant species collected from the karst landscapes of Peñablanca against multi-drug resistant clinical isolates of Staphylococcus aureus and Escherichia coli.

DOI: 10.5281/mcnp.2026.0821 • Pages 1–14
Research Article • Radiologic Technology

Diagnostic Accuracy and Radiation Dose Reduction in Pediatric Chest Digital Radiography Protocols

C. M. Baloran, RRT, MSRT; F. T. Macarubbo, MD

Evaluated the image contrast-to-noise ratio (CNR) and dose-area product (DAP) across 150 pediatric chest radiographic examinations using optimized exposure parameters in secondary and tertiary diagnostic centers in Cagayan Valley.

DOI: 10.5281/mcnp.2026.0822 • Pages 15–28
Research Article • Nursing Practice

Nurse Resilience, Compassion Fatigue, and Patient Care Outcomes in Critical Care Units in Northern Luzon

L. G. Fernandez, RN, PhD; R. T. Pimentel, RN, MAN

A cross-sectional investigation examining structural empowerment, occupational stress markers, and resilience coping mechanisms among 210 registered bedside nurses in public and private medical institutions in Region II.

DOI: 10.5281/mcnp.2026.0823 • Pages 29–42
Research Article • Pharmacy & Pharmacology

Knowledge, Attitudes, and Self-Medication Practices with Antibiotics Among Rural Households in Peñablanca

G. B. Aquino, RPh, MS; K. D. Taguinod, MD

Community-based epidemiological survey evaluating non-prescription antibiotic acquisition habits, risk awareness, and compliance with prescribed dosage regimens among heads of households in seven rural barangays.

DOI: 10.5281/mcnp.2026.0824 • Pages 43–56

Journal Information

  • Review Process: Double-blind peer review
  • Ethics Clearance: Mandatory IERC/PHREB
  • Article Processing Charge (APC): None (Institutional Grant funded)
  • Plagiarism Threshold: Turnitin < 15% similarity
  • Indexing: DOAJ pending, Google Scholar, Philippine E-Journals
