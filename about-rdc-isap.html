Research and Development Center

The Research and Development Center (RDC) of the International School of Asia and the Pacific (ISAP) serves as the institution’s hub for research and scholarly inquiry. It promotes the development of high-quality, ethical, and evidence-based studies in the health and social sciences to support academic excellence, informed decision-making, and community development.

Through research initiatives, capacity-building activities, and collaborative partnerships, the RDC contributes to the advancement of knowledge and innovation while addressing local, national, and global issues. The center remains committed to producing relevant and impactful research that supports the vision and mission of the institution.

Objectives

  1. To develop awareness and evolve a research culture among the stakeholders of education in the school.
  2. To conduct qualitative and quantitative researches relevant to curricular programs of the school contributory to the realization to its general trust.
  3. To develop liberal policies and pro-active programs which shall strengthen the various agenda of the school.
  4. To contribute to the reservoir of knowledge with the end in view of improving existing practices and paradigms.

Research Agenda

A.Y. 2018 – 2022

The ISAP Research Agenda serves as the institution’s guiding framework for research and development initiatives. It was developed through collaborative consultations with academic leaders, faculty members, program coordinators, and stakeholders to ensure alignment with institutional goals and emerging educational, professional, and community needs.

The agenda reflects the institution’s commitment to advancing knowledge, improving academic programs, and contributing to evidence-based practice. It promotes research that is relevant, ethical, and impactful, addressing contemporary issues in health, education, science, technology, management, and community development.

Purposes

  • To give focus and direction on research and development efforts that will address the needs of the college.
  • To serve as a guide to policy makers, funding and donor agencies, and researchers from public and private sectors to invest and ensure productivity and quality of life.
  • To help in providing evidence-based solutions to vital concerns.
  • To serve as basis in maximizing resources for utilization and minimize duplication of research efforts.

Subtopics

A. Promotion and preservation of local heritage in Cagayan

Festivity, cultural dances and songs, food culture, integrated studies for linguistic heritage.

B. Environmental Protection and Conservation

  • Global warming and climate change
  • Energy independence, efficiency and conservation
  • Disaster and risk management: fire, tsunami, earthquake, flood, typhoon
  • Green urban design and waste management
  • Biodiversity conservation in local land use and development planning of Cagayan
  • Governance of parks and public areas

C. Business innovation and opportunities

  • System development
  • Business partnership
  • Research and development of originating products
  • Inventory system
  • Product promotion
  • Logistics and freight forwarding transportation management

D. Health and health services

  • Nutrition, food preparation and planning, food production and innovation
  • Food safety and security
  • Usage of natural resources (plant/animal samples) as drug excipients
  • Dosage formulation and bioassay
  • Antimicrobial analysis, antihypertensive, hypoglycemic, antipyretic, angiogenic/antineoplastic, insecticides, biopesticides

E. Students and Alumni

  • Gender sensitivity
  • Employability of graduates
  • Alumni tracer system
  • Employee morale and organizational climate and culture
  • Job performance and satisfaction
  • Employee engagement
  • Level of satisfaction on services offices and support staff
  • Registrar, business office, library, guidance, laboratories, student services, research and development, extension services, publication, janitorial services
  • Quality of teaching and board exam results/readiness of examinees

F. Management

  • Innovation of equipment
  • Inventory system
  • Queuing system
  • Attendance monitoring system
  • Online learning
  • School website
  • Online student support system
  • Physical plant, PWDs, physical security
  • Human resource management and employee retention

G. Crime, Deviant Behaviour, and Public Safety

  • Economic status, diversity of culture, counter insurgency
  • Student tardiness, study habits, gender sensitivity
  • Barangay profiling system, literary program, licensing and issuance of registration

H. Technology research

  • Arduino-based system
  • Robotics
  • Security and privacy

I. Employees

  • Quality of life and life satisfaction of employees
  • Employee morale, organizational climate and culture
  • Job performance and satisfaction
  • Employee engagement

Vision

The Research and Development Center envisions to be the center of scientific inquiry and program development in the institutions principally in the allied health disciplines and other relevant disciplines which advance the noble vision–mission of the school thus contribute to its development.

Mission

The Research and Development Center is committed to conduct scientific and technological researches to probe existing phenomena in the allied health disciplines and other relevant disciplines and develop progressive policies and responsive programs which address the mandate of this institution.

RDC Standard Operating Procedures

Follow the research process from proposal to publication. Open any required form to access the ISAP registered-forms folder.

1. Apply and prepare for proposal defense

Step

  1. Submit the application and confirm a defense date.
  2. Record and schedule the defense.
  3. Submit the capsule proposal and complete its requirements.
  4. Secure the adviser’s endorsement.

Responsible Person

  • Research teacher / focal person
  • Program coordinator or dean
  • Research coordinator / director
  • RDC secretary and adviser

Requirement

Forms 002–006 ↗
2. Conduct the proposal defense properly

Step

  1. Call the session to order and present the proposal.
  2. Receive clarifications and recommendations.
  3. Panel deliberates and announces the result.
  4. Dismiss the session and record revisions.

Responsible Person

  • Presenters / proponents
  • Research teachers and advisers
  • Panel members

Requirement

Forms 007–009 ↗
3. Secure approval before gathering data

Step

  1. Submit the communication letter and data-gathering tool.
  2. Log documents and forward them to signatories.
  3. Receive approval notification before beginning data gathering.

Responsible Person

  • Proponents
  • RDC secretary
  • Authorized signatories

Requirement

Form 010 ↗
4. Validate the data before analysis

Step

  1. Code or transcribe and validate the gathered data.
  2. Process the statistical treatment fee and submit requirements with receipt.
  3. Complete statistical computation and perform data analysis.

Responsible Person

  • Researcher and statistician
  • RDC secretary
  • Finance

Requirement

Forms 010–011 ↗
5–6. Prepare for and present the final defense

Step

  1. Submit the final-defense application, manuscript, and self-assessment.
  2. Confirm and schedule the defense date.
  3. Present the completed research.
  4. Receive recommendations, document revisions, and record the result.

Responsible Person

  • Researchers, advisers, and research teachers
  • Program coordinator or dean
  • RDC secretary and panel members

Requirement

Forms 002, 005, 006, 009, 012 ↗
7. Submit, check, and approve the final manuscript

Step

  1. Submit, log, and sort the complete manuscript.
  2. RDC staff checks the manuscript and sends the review result.
  3. Complete plagiarism checking, secure approval, and reproduce the manuscript.

Responsible Person

  • Researcher, RDC secretary, and RDC staff
  • Panel members

Requirement

Forms 005, 013 ↗
8. Prepare the research for publication

Step

  1. Convert the manuscript to publication format and submit the self-assessment.
  2. Log and compile the submission, then assign a reviewer.
  3. Complete review, receive acceptance, pay the processing fee, and publish.

Responsible Person

  • Researchers and RDC secretary / staff
  • Reviewer, publication staff, and finance office

Requirement
