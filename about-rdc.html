About the Research & Development Center

The unified academic research engine uniting the Medical Colleges of Northern Philippines and International School of Asia and the Pacific in scientific inquiry, ethical leadership, and transformative community innovation.

Institutional Research Hub

One Center. Two Pillars of Academic Excellence.

The Research and Development Center (RDC) is the shared institutional arm serving both the Medical Colleges of Northern Philippines (MCNP) and the International School of Asia and the Pacific (ISAP). Operating under a unified administrative umbrella at Alimannao Hills, Peñablanca, Cagayan, the RDC drives evidence-based scholarship, oversees clinical and multidisciplinary ethics compliance, cultivates faculty and student research capacity, and translates academic discoveries into sustainable solutions that advance Cagayan Valley and the nation.

By harmonizing health sciences research from MCNP with criminological, technological, educational, and business investigations from ISAP, the RDC champions cross-disciplinary collaboration aligned with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and the National Harmonized Research Agenda.

Allied Health & Clinical Domain

Medical Colleges of Northern Philippines (MCNP) Research Wing

The MCNP Research and Development wing spearheads health-related scientific investigations that improve clinical practice, empower community health workers, and generate evidence-based healthcare policies in Northern Luzon.

Vision

The Research and Development Center envisions to be the center of scientific inquiry and program development in the institutions principally in the allied health disciplines and other relevant disciplines which advance the noble vision–mission of the school, thus contributing to national and global healthcare development.

Mission

The Research and Development Center is committed to conduct scientific and technological researches to probe existing phenomena in the allied health disciplines and develop progressive policies and responsive healthcare programs that address the mandate of the institution and community needs.

Key Research Subtopics & Priorities

Occupational Health & Safety

  • Radiation protection in imaging facilities
  • Ergonomics & employee health safety

Health Promotion Practices

  • Nutrition & non-communicable disease control
  • Maternal, neonatal & geriatric care

Primary Healthcare Systems

  • Rural health information systems
  • Community health worker diagnostics

Community Health Outreach

  • Alagang Dr. Guzman health caravans
  • Disaster risk & emergency rehabilitation

Multidisciplinary & Applied Sciences

International School of Asia and the Pacific (ISAP) Research Wing

The ISAP Research and Development wing fosters pioneering scholarship across criminology, forensic sciences, customs administration, financial management, teacher education, social work, and emerging computing technologies.

Vision

The ISAP Research and Development Center envisions to be a recognized center of applied scientific inquiry, socio-economic policy, and multidisciplinary innovation that advances institutional quality, enhances professional education, and empowers self-sustaining communities in the Asia-Pacific region.

Mission

The ISAP Research and Development Center is committed to generating rigorous, ethical, and solution-driven research across criminological sciences, business, customs, education, and computing disciplines, transforming empirical findings into progressive public policy and community welfare programs.

Research Priority Areas

Criminology & Public Safety

Restorative justice, crime mapping with PNP PRO2, correctional reform, juvenile intervention, and cybercrime defense.

Customs, Trade & Business

Port logistics, tariff administration, digital financial audits, supply chain optimization, and SME innovation.

Pedagogy & Social Development

Curricular efficacy, gender-fair education, mental health support, and community social welfare programs.

Applied Technology & Ecology

Arduino IoT sensor networks, disaster resilience along Cagayan River basin, and renewable energy adoption.

Institutional Research Support

RDC Core Services & Research Ecosystem

Everything faculty researchers, thesis advisers, undergraduate innovators, and postgraduate scholars need to bring high-impact studies from proposal to global indexing.

📋

Ethics Review Clearance

Full committee and expedited ethical evaluations conducted under PHREB Level 1 Accredited standards, safeguarding participant rights, privacy, and research integrity.

💰

Institutional Seed Grants

Competitive research seed grants of up to ₱100,000 per approved project to support faculty research proposals addressing institutional priorities and the UN SDGs.

📊

Statistical Consultation

One-on-one biostatistical and data science support covering experimental design, sample size computation, hypothesis testing, SPSS, R, Jamovi, and qualitative data coding.

🌐

OJS Scholarly Press

Digital manuscript management on Public Knowledge Project's Open Journal Systems (OJS), offering double-blind peer review, permanent digital identifiers, and open access.

🏆

Publication Incentives

Generous cash incentives and merit awards for faculty members and researchers who publish in Scopus, Web of Science, and recognized international indexed journals.

💡

Capability Building & Colloquiums

Regular research workshops, proposal writing bootcamps, plagiarism screening (Turnitin), and institutional colloquiums that mentor researchers at every career stage.

RDC Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

From initial capsule conceptualization to ethics clearance, defense, and final journal publication. Access official downloadable forms for both MCNP and ISAP registered folders.

Step-by-Step SOP Breakdown: Each step is aligned with its designated responsible authority and mandatory documentary requirements.
1. Apply and Prepare for Proposal Defense Phase 01: Orientation & Proposal
Initial phase covering research topic conceptualization, capsule proposal screening, formal defense scheduling in the RDC master calendar, and research adviser endorsement.
Official Downloadable Forms for this Stage:
MCNP Forms 002–006 ↗ ISAP Forms 002–006 ↗
Step & Action Responsible Person Requirement & Deliverables
1.1
Submit Defense Application Accomplish and submit the formal proposal defense application form, propose schedule, and verify proponent eligibility.
Proponent(s) / Research Teacher
Defense Application
  • Defense Application Form (Form 002)
  • Capsule Proposal Draft
  • Curriculum Clearance / Certificate of Enrollment
1.2
Evaluate Proposal & Recommend Panel Assess capsule proposal relevance to institutional agenda and recommend qualified multidisciplinary defense panelists.
Program Coordinator / Dean
Academic Endorsement
  • Proposal Evaluation & Routing Slip
  • Panel Composition Recommendation (Form 003)
1.3
Calendar Defense & Issue Notices Audit completeness of prerequisites, log defense date in master RDC calendar, and transmit formal notices to panel members.
RDC Secretary / Coordinator
Official Notice of Defense
  • Notice of Proposal Defense (Form 004)
  • RDC Master Defense Calendar Entry
  • Panel Invitation Letters
1.4
Adviser Review & Official Endorsement Review final proposal draft (Chapters 1–3) and sign official routing endorsement allowing proponents to proceed to defense.
Research Adviser
Adviser Sign-off
  • Signed Adviser Endorsement Sheet (Form 006)
  • Three (3) Bound Copies of Chapters 1–3 Manuscript
2. Conduct the Proposal Defense Proper Phase 02: Proposal Defense
Formal oral presentation of Chapters 1–3 before the multidisciplinary panel of examiners, evaluation of methodology and conceptual framework, panel deliberation, and issuance of the Matrix of Corrections.
Official Downloadable Forms for this Stage:
MCNP Forms 007–009 ↗ ISAP Forms 007–009 ↗
Step & Action Responsible Person Requirement & Deliverables
2.1
Call Session to Order & Oral Presentation Formally convene defense session and deliver oral presentation of Chapters 1–3 within the allotted 15–20 minutes.
Panel Chair & Proponents
Presentation Deck
  • Proposal Presentation Slide Deck (15–20 mins)
  • Bound Manuscripts for Panelists
  • Defense Program & Attendance Log
2.2
Interpellation & Panel Evaluation Panel members raise critical questions regarding methodology, conceptual framework, sampling design, and ethical feasibility.
Panel Members & Advisers
Evaluation Rubrics
  • Panelist Evaluation Rubric Forms (Form 007)
  • Critique & Annotation Notes on Draft
2.3
Executive Deliberation & Verdict Announcement Panel confers in closed session to calculate average ratings, formulate verdict, and announce official result.
Panel Chair & Panel Members
Deliberation Result
  • Proposal Rating Sheet (Form 008)
  • Official Verdict Sheet (Passed / Passed with Revisions / Re-defense)
2.4
Collate Feedback & Formulate Matrix Record all panel suggestions systematically and formulate the Matrix of Corrections to guide proposal enhancement.
Proponent(s) & Research Adviser
Matrix of Corrections
  • Official Matrix of Corrections (Form 009)
  • Official Minutes of the Defense Session
3. Secure Ethics Clearance & Data-Gathering Permit Phase 03: Ethics Review
Strict ethical clearance requirement before any data collection. Guarantees participant protection, informed consent protocol, and authorized gatekeeper permissions.
Official Downloadable Forms for this Stage:
MCNP Form 010 ↗ ISAP Form 010 ↗
Step & Action Responsible Person Requirement & Deliverables
3.1
Submit Ethics Protocol & Instruments File full protocol dossier including validated survey instruments, interview questionnaires, informed consent, and participant assent forms.
Proponent(s) / Principal Investigator
Protocol Dossier
  • Survey Instrument / Interview Protocol
  • Informed Consent & Assent Forms (English & Local Dialect)
  • Participant Risk Mitigation Plan
3.2
IERC Review & Clearance Certification Institutional Ethics Review Committee evaluates human participant safeguards, data confidentiality, and issues official certification.
Institutional Ethics Review Committee (IERC)
Ethics Certificate
  • IERC Protocol Review Checklist
  • Conflict of Interest Declaration
  • IERC Ethics Clearance Certificate (Form 010)
3.3
Authorize External Gatekeeper Permits Execute official administrative transmittal letters permitting researchers to enter schools, hospitals, agencies, or communities.
Dean, External Gatekeepers & RDC Director
Transmittal Permits
  • Signed Transmittal Letters (Form 010)
  • Authorized Institutional Permit to Collect Data
4. Validate Data & Conduct Statistical Treatment Phase 03: Data & Analysis
Data quality assurance, transcription/coding, processing institutional statistical service fees, and computation of certified descriptive and inferential statistics.
Official Downloadable Forms for this Stage:
MCNP Forms 010–011 ↗ ISAP Forms 010–011 ↗
Step & Action Responsible Person Requirement & Deliverables
4.1
Field Data Collection & Raw Data Cleaning Administer validated instruments in target locales, code raw survey sheets, clean dataset, and detect outliers.
Proponent(s) / Field Enumerators
Dataset & Codebook
  • Retrieved Accomplished Questionnaire Forms
  • Clean Data Spreadsheet (.xlsx / .csv)
  • Variable Codebook & Scoring Key
4.2
Process Statistical Request & Settle Fee Submit dataset and statistical service request with hypotheses to RDC secretariat and settle fee at the cashier.
Proponent(s), RDC Secretary & Finance
Service Request
  • Statistical Treatment Request (Form 011)
  • Official Cashier Payment Receipt
  • Approved Chapter 3 Methodology
4.3
Execute Computations & Statistical Certification Run statistical tests (SPSS / JASP / R), generate statistical cross-tabulations, and deliver certified report.
Institutional Statistician / Data Analyst
Certified Analysis
  • Certified Statistical Analysis Report
  • Statistical Output Tables & Visualizations
  • Statistician’s Clearance Certificate
5–6. Prepare for and Present Oral Final Defense Phase 04: Final Defense
Comprehensive oral defense of the full 5-chapter manuscript, showcasing findings, data interpretations, institutional/clinical implications, and community action plans.
Official Downloadable Forms for this Stage:
MCNP Forms 002, 005, 006, 009, 012 ↗ ISAP Forms 002, 005, 006, 009, 012 ↗
Step & Action Responsible Person Requirement & Deliverables
5.1
Submit Final Defense Application Dossier File application for final oral defense with completed 5-chapter manuscript, statistical clearance, and adviser endorsement.
Proponent(s) & Research Adviser
Final Dossier
  • Final Defense Application (Form 012)
  • Complete 5-Chapter Manuscript
  • Adviser Endorsement Sheet (Form 006)
  • Statistician’s Clearance (Form 011)
5.2
Schedule Defense & Distribute Copies Confirm venue, schedule defense in RDC Calendar, and distribute bound manuscripts to panel members 5 days prior to defense.
Coordinator, Dean & RDC Secretary
Notice & Routing
  • Notice of Final Oral Defense (Form 004)
  • Panel Invitation Letters
  • Manuscript Acknowledgment Receipts
5.3
Conduct Oral Final Defense Presentation Deliver 20–25 minute oral presentation defending findings, interpretations, limitations, and community/policy recommendations.
Proponent(s) before Panel of Examiners
Defense Presentation
  • Final Defense Presentation Deck
  • Demonstration Prototype / System Demo (if applicable)
  • Defense Program & Attendance Log
5.4
Executive Deliberation & Matrix of Corrections Panel calculates final score, determines official verdict, announces result, and itemizes required manuscript modifications.
Panel Chair & Panel Members
Deliberation Result
  • Final Defense Rating Form (Form 008)
  • Official Deliberation Verdict Form
  • Matrix of Final Corrections (Form 009)
7. Plagiarism Clearance, Final Checking & Institutional Approval Phase 04: Manuscript Approval
Verification of panel compliance, Turnitin originality similarity verification ($\le 15\%$), and securing institutional approval signatures for hardbound archiving.
Official Downloadable Forms for this Stage:
MCNP Forms 005, 013 ↗ ISAP Forms 005, 013 ↗
Step & Action Responsible Person Requirement & Deliverables
7.1
Execute Corrections & Secure Verification Integrate all final panel corrections into manuscript and secure individual sign-offs on the compliance matrix from each panelist and adviser.
Proponent(s), Panelists & Adviser
Compliance Matrix
  • Signed Matrix of Corrections (Form 009)
  • Clean Revised Manuscript (Chapters 1–5)
7.2
Turnitin Originality Check Submit digital manuscript for institutional plagiarism check to confirm similarity index adheres to the institutional benchmark (≤ 15%).
RDC Secretariat / Plagiarism Officer
Plagiarism Clearance
  • Turnitin Digital Originality Report (≤ 15%)
  • Certificate of Plagiarism Clearance (Form 013)
7.3
Route Approval Signatures & Hardbound Archive Secure final signatures from Dean and RDC Director on the Approval Sheet, submit 3 hardbound books, and upload archive PDF.
Panel Chair, Academic Dean & RDC Director
Institutional Approval
  • Signed Official Approval Sheet (Form 005)
  • Three (3) Hardbound Books (MCNP Green / ISAP Blue)
  • Archive CD / USB with PDF & Clean Dataset
8. Format Conversion, Peer Review & Journal Publication Phase 05: Publication & Archive
Converting full thesis into standard IMRAD scholarly journal format, double-blind peer review, and formal indexing in MCNP or ISAP peer-reviewed journals.
Official Downloadable Forms for this Stage:
MCNP Forms 014–016 ↗ ISAP Forms 014–016 ↗
Step & Action Responsible Person Requirement & Deliverables
8.1
Reformat to IMRAD Journal Article Condense 5-chapter thesis into a 5,000–8,000 word publishable IMRAD article with structured abstract, keywords, and author declarations.
Author(s) / Proponents
IMRAD Manuscript
  • IMRAD Formatted Manuscript (.docx)
  • Structured Abstract (max 250 words)
  • Author Affiliations & ORCID iDs
  • Author Self-Assessment Checklist (Form 014)
8.2
Submit to Institutional OJS Portal Upload manuscript to MCNP Journal of Medical & Allied Health Sciences or ISAP DAWA Multidisciplinary Journal via OJS.
Corresponding Author & Managing Editor
OJS Submission
  • Active OJS Portal Submission Record
  • Metadata & Author Bio Statements
  • Anonymized Blinded Manuscript File
  • Author Copyright Transfer Agreement (Form 015)
8.3
Double-Blind Peer Review & Publication Undergo double-blind review, address reviewer revisions, finalize galley proofs, register DOI, and publish online.
Peer Reviewers, Managing Editor & Staff
Published Article & DOI
  • Double-Blind Peer Review Evaluation Sheets
  • Official Letter of Acceptance
  • Author-Approved Galley Proof
  • Certificate of Publication (Form 016)
  • Registered CrossRef Digital Object Identifier (DOI)
