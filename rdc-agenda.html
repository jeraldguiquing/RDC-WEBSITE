Strategic Research Agenda (2025–2030)

A unified blueprint anchoring scientific inquiries of MCNP and ISAP to regional priorities, national development goals, and global sustainability standards.

Strategic Direction

The Six Thematic Research Pillars

Formulated through joint academic-industry consultative summits, the RDC Research Agenda provides structured funding, ethics vetting, and mentoring pathways for faculty and student researchers.

01
Lead: MCNP Health Wing UN SDG 3: Good Health & Well-Being NHERA Track 1

Clinical Healthcare Innovation, Disease Surveillance & Diagnostics

Targeting endemic disease epidemiology in Northern Luzon, antimicrobial stewardship, community maternal and pediatric diagnostics, radiologic dose reduction techniques, and hospital clinical safety protocols.

Priority Sub-topics: Phytochemical characterization of native medicinal plants; hospital-acquired infection containment; community point-of-care diagnostics.
02
Lead: ISAP Criminology Wing UN SDG 16: Peace, Justice & Strong Institutions DOST R&D Priority

Public Safety, Forensic Science & Penological Modernization

Developing empirical crime prevention matrices for rural municipalities, forensic ballistic imaging algorithms, restorative jail rehabilitative frameworks, and comparative juvenile justice assessments.

Priority Sub-topics: Community policing trust metrics; questioned document fraud analysis; digital evidence handling under Philippine criminal procedures.
03
Joint: MCNP & ISAP UN SDG 9: Industry, Innovation & Infrastructure

Digital Transformation, Cyber Forensics & Applied Informatics

Advancing artificial intelligence in healthcare triage, cybercrime threat analytics, tele-radiology systems for remote Cagayan islands, and secure institutional information frameworks.

Priority Sub-topics: Predictive clinical diagnostics; ransomware detection for regional organizations; automated customs tariff calculators.
04
Joint: SDG Advocacies Synergy UN SDG 13: Climate Action SDG 11: Sustainable Communities

Cagayan River Basin Eco-Resilience & Disaster Health Management

Investigating flood risk mitigation along the Cagayan River, post-calamity waterborne disease prevention, food security in farming villages, and community mental health during natural disasters.

Priority Sub-topics: Water purification methods for flood-prone barangays; disaster triage readiness among first responders; indigenous coping mechanisms.
05
Lead: Teacher Education & Allied Health UN SDG 4: Quality Education

Pedagogical Dynamics, Simulation Learning & Educational Equity

Measuring learning outcomes in high-fidelity medical and criminology simulation laboratories, gamified learning modules, teacher competence metrics, and inclusive education programs.

Priority Sub-topics: Virtual OSCE (Objective Structured Clinical Examination) assessments; simulation stress tolerance; multilingual education in Cagayan schools.
06
Lead: Customs & Business UN SDG 8: Decent Work & Economic Growth

Trade Logistics, Customs Modernization & Regional Entrepreneurship

Analyzing trade routes connecting Port of Irene, Port of Aparri, and East Asian economic zones, SME survival strategies in Cagayan Valley, and ethical corporate governance.

Priority Sub-topics: Customs digital clearance bottleneck analysis; agricultural supply chain financing; women-led micro-enterprises in Peñablanca.
